Review your own script as a peer reviewer would, also remove bias and look at it with fresh eyes.

**Mechanics first:**

- [ ] Joins that duplicate rows
- [ ] Grain
- [ ] Denominators
- [ ] Date boundaries
- [ ] Filters

**Then reasoning:**

- [ ] Is it the right question?
- [ ] What is outside the frame?
- [ ] Does the "so what" hold up?

**Gate:** give a verdict with **at most three findings**:

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Ship it** | Nothing blocks delivery |
| **Think on it** | Reconsider; the script may change after the review |
| **Fix first** | Correct before anything else |
